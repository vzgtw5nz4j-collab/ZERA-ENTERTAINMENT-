About ZERA

ZERA ENTERTAINMENT is a K‑pop agency focused on talent discovery, training, production, and global promotion. We provide dance, vocal, language, and media coaching to help artists reach international audiences.

Mission

Develop unique voices and performances that connect with fans around the world.

Programs

  • Training: dance, vocals, performance, languages
  • Production: songwriting and studio production
  • Promotion: campaigns, tours, and digital distribution
